Before publishing
-----------------
1. Open config.js and replace YOUR_WHATSAPP_NUMBER with your WhatsApp Business number in international format.
2. Replace/extend the footer with your final business/legal details.
3. Add Privacy Policy, Terms and a final cancellation policy before collecting personal data or payments publicly.
4. Test the site on iPhone + Android.
5. Host for free on Netlify, Vercel or GitHub Pages, or use your chosen provider.
