The problem

A listing shows you the apartment. A visit shows you the truth.

Photos rarely show street noise, common areas, poor light, visible humidity, damaged furniture or what is outside the window. YoVoy gives you the information you would collect if you were physically in Madrid.

FAQ

Before you send a listing.

Are you a real estate agency?

No. YoVoy is an independent viewing and concierge service working for the person requesting the check.

Can you arrange the viewing?

Yes. With the listing and contact details, we can coordinate the viewing time with the agency or landlord.

Can I join by video call?

Yes. Choose Full Check + Live and join the viewing remotely.

Do you guarantee the apartment or the landlord?

No. We document what we can observe during the visit and relay information provided by the agent or landlord. We do not certify ownership, legality or hidden defects.

Can you review a rental contract?

We can help organize and summarize the document. Legal advice or formal legal review should be handled by a qualified professional.
